The Journal CloudMeet the character

Meet Journal

Journal is a small needle-felted cloud with two glossy black eyes and no mouth. It never speaks: it shows how it feels through shape, tilt, light and a few glowing marks drawn in the air.

Form
One big central puff with a domed top, side puffs left and right, a small upper-right puff, a softly bumped underside
Material
Felted wool: soft, slightly fuzzy fibres with natural variation
Eyes
Two smooth, slightly glossy black ovals; no mouth
Light
Backlit glow around the whole silhouette, warm in the creases
Proportions
Height about 0.7 × width · each eye about 12% of width · eyes about 20% of width apart

Colour palette

Main cloud#F5EDE1 · Warm white (cream)
Shadow#E6D8C7 · Soft beige
Warm light#FFD9A6 · Sunset glow, highlight
Eye#1A1A1A · Deep black, glossy

The Journal CloudExpressions

Mood through shape, tilt and light

Journal has no mouth. Moods come from the body first: rounder for happy, a tilt for curious, a little stretch for excited, eyes lower for sleepy. Light-beam marks are added on top when a feeling needs to read from far away.

The Journal CloudLight-beam elements

Indicators and symbols

Drawn in lightEvery mark is a thin warm-gold stroke (#FFD9A6) with a soft glow, never a solid shape.
Close to the bodyMarks sit around the head or orbit the cloud; they never cover the eyes.
One idea at a timeUse a single symbol per moment so the feeling reads at a glance.
No wordsSymbols replace text: a question mark, never the word “why”.

The Journal CloudMotion

Actions and movement

Actions with light trails

Animation sequence: sit still, float up, turn around

Storyboard for the 10-second animation

Motion is slow and buoyant: Journal eases into every move, floats rather than jumps, and settles with a small overshoot. Light trails show direction (an arrow for lift, a ring for rotation) and fade as the move ends.

The Journal CloudEnvironments

One companion, every kind of sky

The felt body keeps its cream colour everywhere; the world tints it. Daylight is neutral, golden hour adds peach in the creases, night turns the glow lavender, and rain greys the wool down.

The Journal CloudApp icon

Possible app icons

The icon reduces Journal to its silhouette and its glow: a single soft cloud shape lit from inside, with no eyes, so it stays readable at small sizes. Four background options are under consideration.
